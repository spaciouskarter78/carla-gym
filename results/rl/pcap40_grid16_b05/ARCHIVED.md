# Archived: pcap trace scenario

These 10 runs (PPO and SAC, 5 seeds, medium budget, 0.4.0 simulator) were
trained on `data/pcap_trace.csv`, a packet-capture trace whose origin and
licence could not be established. The trace was removed from the repository
in favour of SNDlib's Abilene data (`data/ABILENE_TRACE.md`), and the
scenario was replaced by `abilene_l120_b08` (`docs/ABILENE_SCENARIO.md`).

The results are kept as a record of what was reported at the time
(`docs/RL_RESULTS_0.4.0.md`, `docs/E1_E4_RESULTS.md`). They cannot be
reproduced from this repository any more and should not be reported in a
paper.
